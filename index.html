NEXUS 2040 is a concept demonstration built for a school exhibition on India's Vision 2040 — a technologically advanced, sustainable urban future. It simulates a fictional smart city, "Nexus City," showing how transparent rule-based systems empower city operators.

Simulated Proof of Concept: This software uses deterministic if/else logic, not a black-box model. It does not read real-world sensors or predict external events. All data is synthetically generated in your browser.
